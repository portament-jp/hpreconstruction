Handling of Google User Data

OpsGuide accesses Google user data through Google APIs only when you connect your Google account and set up OpsGuide flows (automated processes) to use Google services. For Google user data, the provisions of this section take precedence over the other provisions of this Privacy Policy.

Data We Access

With your permission, we access the following data.

  • Basic Google account information: email addresses and name (to identify the connected account)
  • Gmail: reading, organizing (such as labeling), sending and deleting email (using full Gmail access)
  • Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets: reading, creating, updating and deleting files (for the files specified in your flows)
  • Google Calendar: reading, creating, updating and deleting events (using full Google Calendar access)
  • Google Chat: reading, creating, updating and deleting spaces, members and messages (including managing your organization's spaces with administrator privileges, and viewing and updating read status)
  • Google Forms: reading form content and responses, and creating, updating and deleting forms (used to retrieve the content and responses of the forms specified in your flows, and to register notifications that detect new responses)
  • Google Contacts and Google Workspace directory: reading contacts and user information to search for and specify recipients
  • YouTube: uploading the video specified in your flow to your YouTube channel, with its title and description. We do not view, edit or delete your existing videos

Purpose of Use

We use Google user data only to provide you with the features you configure in OpsGuide (such as running flows, detecting triggers, and displaying execution results and execution history), and to investigate and fix problems with those features. We do not use it for advertising (including personalized, retargeting and interest-based advertising), marketing, promotion, credit decisions or any other purpose unrelated to the features provided to you.

Sharing and Transfer

We do not sell Google user data. We do not share or transfer Google user data to third parties except in the following cases.

  • To carry out the processing you configure in your flows (such as sending email to recipients you specify, or passing data to external services you add to your flows)
  • When we entrust processing to service providers necessary to operate OpsGuide (such as cloud infrastructure), under our instructions and subject to protections equivalent to this section
  • When necessary to comply with laws, or to ensure security, such as investigating and preventing abuse
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition or business transfer (we will notify you in advance and require the successor to handle the data in a manner equivalent to this section)

Human Access

Our officers and employees do not read Google user data, except (a) where you have explicitly consented to our viewing specific data, for example for support, (b) where necessary for security purposes, such as investigating abuse, (c) where necessary to comply with laws, or (d) where data aggregated and anonymized so that individuals cannot be identified is used for internal operations.

No Use for AI/ML Model Training

We do not use Google user data (including data obtained through Google Workspace APIs) to develop, improve or train general-purpose AI/ML models, even in anonymized or aggregated form, and we do not allow third parties to do so. If you add AI processing to a flow, Google user data is sent to the AI service only to carry out that processing.

Storage and Deletion

Google user data is processed to the extent necessary to run your flows, and the results are stored as execution history so that you can review them. Access tokens used to connect with Google are stored in an access-restricted environment.

You can disconnect your Google account from "Settings → Connected services" in OpsGuide, and you can revoke OpsGuide's access at any time from your Google Account settings page. If you would like stored data to be deleted, please contact us using the details below. When you delete your account or request deletion, we will delete the data promptly, except where retention is required by law.

Limited Use

OpsGuide's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
